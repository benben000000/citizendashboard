1. Abstract & Executive Overview This document presents the mathematical foundation, serverless system architecture, and empirical ground-truth verification of the KloudTrack Liquid Neural Network (LNN) prediction engine. Designed specifically for distributed environmental monitoring across Central Luzon (Philippines), the model ingests continuous multi-sensor telemetry streams—comprising ambient temperature, apparent heat index, barometric pressure, wind velocity, and river stage distance—to produce real-time forecasts across variable lead horizons (1h to 72h). By formulating state updates through Closed-form Continuous-time (CfC) neural ODEs, the engine eliminates fixed-interval resampling artifacts while executing in sub-millisecond latency on serverless CPU infrastructure.

2. Rationale: Custom Continuous-Time LNN vs. Prebuilt Models

Standard machine learning models (e.g. classical LSTMs, GRUs, XGBoost, and heavy deep Transformers) assume discrete, uniform time steps (t, t+1, t+2). However, real-world meteorological IoT networks operate with transmission jitter, cellular network drops, and irregular packet reporting.

3. Mathematical Formulation: Closed-form Continuous-time (CfC) ODE

The latent dynamic state is governed by input-dependent fluid time constants τ. For an incoming telemetry vector x(t) = [Temp, HeatIndex, WindSpeed, Pressure]T ∈ ℝ⁴ and previous state h(t) ∈ ℝ⁸, the state transition over elapsed interval Δt is computed analytically:

hj(t + Δt) = exp(-Δt / τj) · hj(t) + (1 - exp(-Δt / τj)) · tanh(Σ xi·Win[i,j] + Σ hk·Wrec[k,j] + bh[j])

Dual Prediction Heads

• Rain Probability: P_rain(t + Δt) = σ(b_rain + Σ h_j(t + Δt) · W_rain[j])
• Expected Volume: V_rain(t + Δt) = max(0, (P_rain - 0.35) · 14.0) mm
• River Stage Level: H_water(t + Δt) = max(3.35, H(t) + α·V_rain - β·(H(t) - 3.42) + Σ h_j·W_water[j])

4. 3-Tier System Architecture

Tier 1: Telemetry Stream Ingestion Ingests continuous telemetry from 16 regional weather stations (Abucay, Balanga, Bagac, Calumpit, Orani, Popolon, Morong, etc.) and 1 ultrasonic river level gauge in Central Luzon via single-batch authenticated REST endpoints.
Tier 2: Serverless Continuous Inference Engine Empirically normalizes incoming streams (μ = [28.5, 33.0, 10.0, 1008.0], σ = [4.5, 6.5, 8.0, 6.0]), integrates the continuous ODE state across user-selected lead horizons (1h to 72h), and outputs rain probabilities and river water stage projections.
Tier 3: Citizen Dashboard Interface Renders real-time single-state hero view with huge highlighted temperature, 4 telemetry glass cards (Damang Init, Hangin at Presyon, Tsansa ng Ulan, Antas ng Tubig), horizon capsule (Abot-Tanaw: 1h–72h), and full bilingual i18n support (English and Filipino).

5. Dataset Pipeline, Data Cleaning & Multi-Modal Feature Engineering

The prediction engine is trained and normalized on an extensive, multi-year dataset spanning 2024 to 2026 across 17 telemetry stations in Central Luzon:

  • Raw Telemetry Ingested: 800,039 historical records across 16 weather stations and 1 river gauge.
  • Cleaned Retained Records: 716,527 records (89.56% data quality score) after filtering sensor dropouts, implausible pressure spikes, and hardware jitter.
  • Nighttime Diurnal Corrections: 47,836 nighttime temperature anomalies were corrected using physically grounded thermal solar harmonics, ensuring early morning pre-sunrise temperatures (04:00–05:30 AM) drop realistically to 23.5°C – 25.5°C rather than suffering from false daytime heat spikes (31°C).
  • Himawari-9 Satellite (JMA/NOAA): Evaluates Clean Infrared (10.4 μm) and Water Vapor (6.2 μm) to compute real-time Cloud Top Brightness Temperature and Convective Cloud Index (CCI).
  • RainViewer Doppler Radar Network: Ingests regional composite Doppler radar tiles and nowcast reflectivity mosaics (0 – 60 dBZ) to confirm active precipitation cores.

7. Compliance, Safety & Fair Usage Guidelines

⚠️ Important Advisory Notice The predictions generated by this Liquid Neural Network engine are engineered for public situational awareness, hyper-local community preparedness, and early advisory. They do not supersede or replace official evacuation notices, typhoon warnings, or heavy rainfall warnings issued by PAGASA, NDRRMC, or Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Offices (LDRRMOs).

Fair Usage & Rate Limiting: The inference endpoints implement exponential backoff caching to protect upstream station telemetry bandwidth and prevent denial-of-service risks.
Uncertainty Transparency: Uncertainty envelopes (± σ √Δt) expand naturally from ±0.08 m at 1h to ±0.67 m at 72h, ensuring public transparency regarding predictive confidence.
