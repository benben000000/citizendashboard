## Reading this table

`skill` is MAE improvement against **persistence** on the identical
rows. It is not a comparison against NWP. A cell can show strong
skill and still be beaten by every NWP model; a cell can be nearly
tied with persistence and still beat all seven NWP models. Both have
happened here, so the two columns are reported separately.

A skill value of about 0.0 means the residual head contributes
essentially nothing at that horizon and the model is *returning
persistence*. Those cells are not wins; they are unused capacity.
