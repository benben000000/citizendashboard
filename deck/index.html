Hyper-local nowcasting for the fifteen minutes that decide whether a street floods

Sixteen ground stations, a continuous-time liquid neural network, and an open benchmark against seven operational NWP models — published with its caveats.

Every figure in this deck is read from prediction-model/data/nwp_benchmark_production.json — test split 2026-08-15 → 2026-08-26, 16 stations, opened once.

The gap

Global models forecast 25 km squares every six to twelve hours. A cloudburst floods a street in fifteen minutes.

0.25°
ECMWF and GFS grid resolution — roughly 25 km across. An entire barangay reads one forecast value.
6–12 h
Model update cycle. Every decision made between cycles is unobserved and unforecast.
1–5 km
Our station spacing — dense enough to catch the convective cell that actually causes the flood.

We are not trying to beat a global model at its own game. We are trying to own the gap between its update cycles.

System

Sixteen stations, a continuous-time model, and a gate that decides when to trust it.

01 / PHYSICAL

Ground stations

16 automated weather stations. One-minute MQTT over mutual TLS. 756,156 raw rows ingested since June.

02 / QUALITY CONTROL

Quarantine

22,955 rows rejected on physical bounds and year-range rules before anything is trained.

03 / MODEL

Closed-form CfC

decay = e−Δt·w. 24-step window, 75 context features computed at origin only.

04 / POLICY GATE

Source selection

Per-variable, per-horizon routing. 15 of 20 benchmarked cells serve persistence.

05 / CITIZEN API

Edge delivery

Next.js route handlers, 13.7–17.6 ms. Every response stamped with policy version and commit.

The gate is the point. A model trusted only where it measurably wins is a product; a model trusted everywhere is a liability.

Where we lose

We lose to ECMWF on temperature at +6 h and +12 h.

At +6 h we beat persistence by 13.0% and lose to ECMWF (1.477 vs 1.274). ECMWF also has the lowest MAE of any method at +1 h and +3 h — where the policy serves persistence, not the network.
Why the remaining numbers can be trusted

Seven silent defects, each of which produced a number we believed.

01NWP wind units km/h compared against m/s — every NWP wind error inflated 3.6×. We appeared 6× better than ECMWF when we were about 1.4× better.
02NWP window selection Longest-wins cache selection picked a window with zero overlap with the test period. All seven models scored n/a — and the report still printed a rank.
03Candidate input space Candidates were scored on de-normalised input. +12 h temperature “degraded 35.7%” and the architecture was rejected on a harness bug.
04Promotion audit arity A missing tuple field raised on every run, silently skipping the promotion audit while leaving complete-looking artifacts on disk and a non-zero exit code.
05Bias sign Benchmark and dashboard used opposite conventions — the same forecast read as over-predicting on one screen and under-predicting on the other.
06ReLU wind floor Zero gradient on the negative side. The head learned −5.75 m/s and 70% of windows clamped to 0.0 — a plausible calm forecast from a dead unit.
07Seed sweep measured nothing Both arms loaded the same model.py. Two architectures matched to six decimals — roughly six hours of compute produced no result.

All seven are written up in prediction-model/docs/known-limitations.md §6, each with the check that now catches it.

What moves the numbers

Six items, ordered by measured headroom rather than by effort.

01 Re-run the wind benchmark with the three dead anemometers excluded — the margin needs to be the real margin gating
02 Test +24 h at offset horizons (+18 h, +30 h) to settle the diurnal-phase hypothesis gating
03 Open the humidity gate selectively — the head shows real skill at +12 h and we are discarding all of it +19.7% @ +12h
04 Re-run the seed sweep with verified per-seed variance, to size the run-to-run noise band noise floor
05 Repair or retire wkAWlzlm — a rostered station whose history endpoint answers with zero rows data
06 Revisit pressure only against a product that publishes absolute mean-sea-level pressure pressure

Until items 01 and 02 run, no external claim about wind skill or +24 h skill is supportable.

The position

A genuine short-lead result, published with its caveats.

What is true
  • Rain occurrence ranks 1st of 10, +1 h to +12 h
  • Wind beats all seven NWP models at +6 h and +12 h
  • Seven silent defects found, fixed and regression-guarded
What is not
  • Not a replacement for ECMWF on temperature
  • Not forecasting pressure or humidity — those serve persistence
  • Not for life safety; flood and evacuation claims are out of the core
What we need
  • Fund gating items 01 and 02
  • Doppler radar reflectivity as an independent verification source
  • Three replacement anemometers and gauges at mountain-foot sites
Until the rain margin survives +24 h and the wind margin survives a repaired ground truth, this is a decision-support prototype — and we will keep saying so.

Deep dive — researcher

A closed-form continuous-time cell, and a protocol built so leakage is impossible.

w      = softplus(W_time · [x ; h])
decay = exp(−Δt · w)
h′   = decay · h + (1 − decay) · σ(W_gate · [x ; h]) · tanh(W_state · [x ; h])
  • Continuous time is the point: a one-minute gap and a three-hour gap produce different decays, and the ingest is irregular one-minute data binned to hours with holes.
  • 8 sequence features plus 75 context features, all computed at origin t0 only. Nothing in the context vector derives from the target hour.
  • Zero-initialised residual heads: at initialisation the network is persistence. Training must earn every departure, so a failed run degrades to persistence rather than to noise.
  • Rain is modelled in two stages — occurrence × conditional amount — so the ~57% of dry hours do not wash out the heavy-rain response.
chronological 60/20/20 · 48 h embargo (seq_len 24 + max_horizon 24) · normalisation fitted on TRAIN only · test split read exactly once

Persistence error peaks at 12–14 h, then falls. We can explain that. We have not tested it.

All four variables peak near 12–14 h and decline toward 24 h on an identical origin set, so window composition cannot explain it. Consistent with a strong ~24-hour periodic component: a 24-hour lead returns to the same diurnal phase and partially cancels the anti-phase maximum at 12 h.

  • Untested. The test is to re-benchmark at +18 h and +30 h. Until it runs, every +24 h figure in the repository is provisional.
  • Humidity headroom of +19.7% at +12 h exists and is discarded, because the promotion rule demands skill on a minimum number of horizons at once.
  • The seed-sweep noise band is unmeasured, so no effect smaller than it can honestly be promoted.

Deep dive — mechanical engineer

Sixteen stations on the model corpus, twenty-three across the wider network.

Measured channels Temperature, relative humidity, barometric pressure, wind speed and direction, precipitation (tipping bucket).
Barometric standardisation Readings are reduced to mean-sea-level pressure with the WMO hypsometric relation. This is what stops mountain stations tripping false cyclone alerts.
Rain rate is not rain depth Gauges report mm/h. 8.0 mm/h for 15 minutes is 2.0 mm on the ground. Summing raw snapshots instead of integrating reports four times the water that fell.
Water level Ultrasonic gauges (e.g. Calumpit WLMS O3z0j5bG on the Pampanga/Angat). Currently an internal beta experiment, flagged not_for_life_safety and excluded from the commercial core.
Not scored UV index reports 11.0 at midnight; light intensity is an uncalibrated photometric sensor with no orientation metadata. Both are excluded from skill claims rather than smoothed over.
